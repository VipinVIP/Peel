Peel puts stickers on your home screen. Pick a GIF or WebP, animated or still, and tap the sticker to play it.

Transparent parts of the image show your wallpaper, so there is no box or background: just the sticker.

Features:
• Truly transparent home-screen widget
• Tap to play; choose 1–50 plays per tap, or loop until tapped again
• Several stickers can play at the same time
• GIF and WebP, animated or still, up to 40 MB
• Material You design with dynamic color, light and dark theme

Privacy: Peel has no internet permission, ads or tracking. Your images are processed on your phone and never leave it.
